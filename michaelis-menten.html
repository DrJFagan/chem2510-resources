Inhibitor

With inhibitor: $V_0 = \dfrac{V_{\max}[\mathrm{S}]_0}{\alpha K_m + \alpha'[\mathrm{S}]_0}$, where $\alpha = 1 + [\mathrm{I}]/K_I$ and $\alpha' = 1 + [\mathrm{I}]/K_I'$. $K_I$ is the dissociation constant for inhibitor binding to free enzyme ($\mathrm{E{\cdot}I}$) and $K_I'$ is for binding to the enzyme–substrate complex ($\mathrm{ES{\cdot}I}$). Competitive uses $K_I$ only, uncompetitive uses $K_I'$ only, noncompetitive sets $K_I' = K_I$, and mixed lets you set both.
